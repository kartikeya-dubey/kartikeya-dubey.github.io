How it works

Five steps from reel to library.

01

Send

Share a reel or post with @getfol.io on Instagram, or paste a link. Shares from any chat work.

02

Keep

We save it straight away, so nothing gets lost along the way.

03

Understand

We read the caption and your note to work out what it is about: a title, a short summary, tags and places.

04

Sort

Each save goes into a group like Travel or Food. If nothing fits, we make a new one.

05

Reply

You get a short message back on Instagram: saved, sorted, or what we need from you.

What you keep

Links and meaning, not media.

We keep the link, the caption and a short summary of what the post is about. We never copy the video, audio or images, so the original stays with its creator.

What it adds

Structure from a caption.

A travel reel yields places and duration; a recipe yields dishes and ingredients; a product yields names and attributes. Items filed together make a library you can actually search.

What stays yours

Private by default.

Your library is yours alone. No one else sees what you save, and you can download or delete everything whenever you like.

Questions.

Who is Fol.io for?

Anyone who saves a lot of Instagram content and wants it findable later: travel ideas, recipes, products, workouts, study material.

Does it store my videos?

No. We keep the link, the caption and a short summary of what the post is about. The original stays on Instagram, and no video, audio or image is copied.

Can other users see my saves?

No. Your saves, notes and clusters are private to you.

How do I get access?

Fol.io is in early testing and free for volunteers. Fill in the short volunteer form and we will contact you to help set up your access. After that you can create your account.

Is this connected to DexAI?

Fol.io grew out of what I learned building DexAI, my earlier project. Different product, same care. See DexAI.
