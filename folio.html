How it works

Five steps from reel to library.

01

Send

DM a reel or post to @getfol.io, or paste a link. Shares from any chat work.

Sender is recognised from the handle you set.
02

Store

The message is stored first and acknowledged at once, so nothing is lost if a step fails.

Duplicates are dropped at the door.
03

Understand

The caption and your note become a title, summary, tags, places and products.

Behind a swappable AI provider.
04

Organise

Each item is filed into a cluster, like Travel or Recipes. New clusters appear when needed.

Per-user, never shared.
05

Reply

You get a short text back: saved, filed, or what extra info is needed.

Each reply is sent once.
What you keep

Links and meaning, not media.

Fol.io stores the link to the original, the caption and what its AI understood from it. It does not download or keep video, audio or images, which keeps it light and respects the original creator. Cards show a coloured cover instead.

What it adds

Structure from a caption.

A travel reel yields places and duration; a recipe yields dishes and ingredients; a product yields names and attributes. Items filed together make a library you can actually search.

What stays yours

Private by default.

A post is read once and its reading shared across users, but each person's saves, clusters and notes are theirs alone, enforced in the database with row-level security. A save with a personal note is read privately and never shared.

Architecture

Small, bursty, event-shaped work.

Edge receiver

A Cloudflare Worker at a fixed address verifies the Instagram signature, stores the event and replies in milliseconds.

Cloudflare Worker

Stored inbox

Events wait in Postgres and are claimed in batches with row locks. A restart loses nothing.

Supabase Postgres

Stateless API

FastAPI runs as Vercel functions in Singapore, close to the database. No idle server to keep alive.

FastAPI · Vercel

Caption AI

Batched, cost-capped understanding with a circuit breaker, stored per item and behind a provider interface.

Gemini · swappable

A library that knows what is in it.

Saving is easy and finding is not. Most people have hundreds of saved reels they never open again, because nothing tells them what each one holds.

Fol.io treats the content as the point. It reads what a post is about, files it for you and lets you come back by topic, place or product instead of by memory.

Questions.

Who is Fol.io for?

Anyone who saves a lot of Instagram content and wants it findable later: travel ideas, recipes, products, workouts, study material.

Does it store my videos?

No. It keeps the link, the caption and what the AI understood from the caption. The original stays on Instagram, and no video, audio or image is copied.

Can other users see my saves?

No. A post is understood once and shared internally, but your saves, notes and clusters are private to you and protected in the database itself.

How do I get access?

Fol.io is in early access. Create an account, set your Instagram handle and an admin confirms you for testing.

Is this connected to DexAI?

Fol.io started from DexAI's patterns (FastAPI, Postgres, React) and then moved to a lighter, event-driven design that suits bursty message work. See DexAI.
